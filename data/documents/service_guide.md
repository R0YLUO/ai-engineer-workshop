# Swift Freight Co. — Customer Service Guide

Version 4.2 · Effective 1 July 2026 · Internal and customer-facing reference

## About Swift Freight

Swift Freight Co. is an Australian delivery services company founded in 2011. We move parcels, pallets and bulky goods for more than 2,000 business customers across New South Wales, Victoria and Queensland. Our fleet of 340 vehicles includes vans, rigid trucks and temperature-controlled vehicles, operating from depots in Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane.

Our customers are mostly retailers, pharmacies, furniture makers and wholesalers. Every customer has a service contract that sets their own rates. Where a contract is silent, the standard terms in this guide apply.

## Service Levels

We offer three service levels. The service level is chosen by the customer at the time of booking and cannot be changed after the goods are collected.

**Standard delivery** is delivered within 2 business days for metropolitan routes and within 4 business days for regional routes. Standard delivery is our most economical option and is suitable for most non-urgent goods.

**Express delivery** is delivered by the end of the next business day for metropolitan and inter-city routes, and within 2 business days for regional routes. Express bookings must be made before 2:00 pm local time to qualify for next-day delivery.

**Same-day delivery** is available within the Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane metropolitan areas only. Bookings must be made before 11:00 am. Same-day delivery is priced individually by quote and is not covered by standard contract rates.

## How Pricing Works

Every delivery is priced by distance. The base charge for a delivery is the distance in kilometres multiplied by the customer's contracted rate per kilometre for the service level that was booked.

Base charge = distance (km) × contracted rate per km for the booked service level

Applicable surcharges are then added to each delivery. Finally, any contracted discount is applied to the invoice total.

Invoice total = (sum of base charges + surcharges) × (1 − contracted discount %)

Our list rates, used when a customer has no contract, are $3.20 per km for standard delivery and $5.20 per km for express delivery. Distances are measured using the shortest practical road route between the collection and delivery addresses, rounded to the nearest kilometre. A minimum charge of $45.00 applies to every delivery.

Contract rates are reviewed every 12 months. Customers are notified in writing at least 30 days before any rate change takes effect.

## Volume Discounts

Customers who commit to more than 200 deliveries per quarter may negotiate a volume discount of between 5% and 15%. The agreed discount percentage is recorded in the customer's contract and applies to the whole invoice total, including surcharges. Discounts are not applied to same-day quotes.

## Surcharges

**Refrigerated surcharge.** Temperature-controlled deliveries attract a flat refrigerated surcharge per delivery. The list price is $30.00 per delivery; contracted customers may have a different amount.

**Heavy item surcharge.** Any single item weighing more than 100 kg attracts a heavy item surcharge of $40.00 per delivery. This covers the two-person crew and tail-lift vehicle needed for safe handling.

**After-hours surcharge.** Deliveries requested between 7:00 pm and 6:00 am, or on weekends and public holidays, attract an after-hours surcharge of 25% of the base charge.

**Dangerous goods surcharge.** Approved dangerous goods consignments attract a surcharge of $55.00 per delivery to cover compliant handling, placarding and documentation.

**Fuel levy.** We do not currently charge a separate fuel levy. Fuel costs are included in the per-kilometre rate.

## Refrigerated (Temperature-Controlled) Delivery

Our refrigerated service is designed for pharmaceuticals, vaccines, fresh food and other temperature-sensitive goods. Refrigerated vehicles maintain a controlled range of 2°C to 8°C for the entire journey. A frozen range of −18°C or colder is available on request for metropolitan routes only.

Every refrigerated vehicle carries calibrated data loggers that record the cargo temperature every 5 minutes. A full temperature log for each delivery is available to the customer on request within 90 days of delivery, at no extra cost. If the temperature leaves the agreed range for more than 15 minutes, the driver is alerted and our operations centre contacts the customer immediately.

Refrigerated delivery is available at both standard and express service levels and is priced as the base charge plus the refrigerated surcharge. Goods must be pre-conditioned to the correct temperature before collection.

## Dangerous Goods

We carry a limited range of dangerous goods in accordance with the Australian Dangerous Goods Code. Accepted classes include Class 2.1 and 2.2 gases (such as aerosols and small gas cylinders up to 9 kg), Class 3 flammable liquids in limited quantities (such as paints and solvents) and Class 9 miscellaneous goods (such as lithium batteries packed with equipment).

We do not carry explosives (Class 1), toxic or infectious substances (Class 6), radioactive material (Class 7) or bulk dangerous goods. All dangerous goods must be declared at the time of booking with a completed Dangerous Goods Declaration and correctly labelled packaging. Undeclared dangerous goods will be returned to the sender at the sender's cost.

Dangerous goods cannot be combined with refrigerated or same-day services.

## Cancellations and Changes

Bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to 4 hours before the scheduled collection time. Cancellations made less than 4 hours before collection incur a cancellation fee of $35.00. If our driver has already arrived at the collection address, a failed collection fee equal to 50% of the base charge applies.

Delivery addresses can be changed before collection at no cost. Changes after collection are treated as a redelivery and are charged as a new delivery from the point where the goods are held.

## Claims for Loss or Damage

Customers must report visible damage within 48 hours of delivery and concealed damage within 7 days. Claims are lodged through the customer portal with photos and the original invoice for the goods. Our standard liability is limited to $1,000 per consignment unless the customer has purchased additional transit insurance at the time of booking. Claims are assessed within 10 business days.

## Billing and Payment Terms

Invoices are issued weekly, every Monday, for all deliveries completed in the previous week. Each invoice line shows the booking ID, route, distance, service level, rate applied, surcharges and any discount. Payment terms are 30 days from the invoice date. Late payments may attract interest of 1.5% per month.

## Invoice Disputes

If a customer believes an invoice is incorrect, they should contact the finance team within 60 days of the invoice date, quoting the invoice number. The finance team will compare the invoice against the booking record and the customer's contract. If an error is confirmed, a credit note or corrected invoice is issued within 5 business days and the customer does not need to pay the disputed amount in the meantime. Undisputed amounts remain payable on the normal terms.

## Coverage Area

We service all metropolitan and regional areas of New South Wales, Victoria and south-east Queensland. Remote areas west of the Great Dividing Range are serviced twice weekly, on Tuesdays and Thursdays. We do not currently deliver to Tasmania, South Australia, Western Australia or the Northern Territory.
